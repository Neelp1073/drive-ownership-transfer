1. Setup 2. Sign in 3. Scan 4. Review 5. Transfer

1. Setup

Create an OAuth client in Google Cloud, then paste the client ID here. Tokens are stored only in this browser profile.

2. Sign in

Phase 1 / Workspace: sign in as the source (current owner). Phase 2: sign out, then sign in as the target to accept pending transfers. Click Sign in once and wait for Google’s window — don’t click again if a popup is already open.

3. Scan My Drive

Lists every non-trashed item you own so you can search and pick later. Nothing is transferred from this scan until you add specific files or folders in step 4. Transferring a folder does not move ownership of the files inside it, so adding a folder also selects the owned files and subfolders in it.

    What this tool will not do

    • Shared Drive files have no personal owner, so they cannot be transferred.
    • Google blocks transfers across different Workspace organizations.
    • Google blocks personal Gmail ↔ Workspace ownership transfers.
    • Items you do not own, and items the API marks as not shareable, are skipped.
    • For Gmail-to-Gmail, the target must sign in here and accept. Until then, ownership has not changed.
    • The old owner is never removed as a writer. Nothing is deleted or downloaded.
